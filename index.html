How to Use ZIVVI

Start managing your finances in just a few simple steps. No complexity, just smart insights.

Step 1

Create Your Account

Get started by creating your account in just a few seconds. Set your monthly budget and preferences to begin tracking your expenses in a structured way.

The registration process is quick and simple — just enter your name, email address, and create a secure password to access your personalized dashboard and start managing your finances instantly.

Step 2

Add Your Transactions

Start by adding your daily expenses like food, travel, bills, or shopping. Simply enter the amount, choose a category, and save it — ZIVVI will automatically organize everything for you.

You don’t need to worry about managing data manually. Every transaction is tracked in real-time, helping you clearly see where your money is going and how much you are spending every day.

Step 3

Get AI Insights

Once your data is added, ZIVVI analyzes your spending patterns using smart AI. It identifies where you're overspending, highlights unusual expenses, and gives helpful suggestions to improve your financial habits.

You will get clear insights like how much you can save, where to cut costs, and how to stay within your budget — making it easier to take control of your money without any guesswork.
